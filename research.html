Research

Multiscale structure as a window into nonlinear dynamics.

I study how nonlinear multiphysics interactions generate structure across scales, and how those structures reveal the dynamical states of complex systems. The interstellar medium provides a natural laboratory, while MHD, turbulent fluids, multiscale reconstruction, scale-aware intervention, morphological comparison and representation learning provide complementary routes into the same problem.

01 · Representation learning

ScaleAware-JEPA: toward physical world models

ScaleAware-JEPA learns dense, label-free latent coordinates for continuous physical fields by making the field’s own multiscale hierarchy part of the predictive task. In the MHD application, density morphology alone produces a latent atlas that is systematically organized by post-training dynamical diagnostics. The broader direction is toward learned physical state spaces with world-model-like predictive structure.

02 · Scale-aware XAI

Scale-Aware XAI framework

Standard pixel perturbations can leave the manifold of physically valid states. The scale-aware framework instead reconstructs a physical baseline, decomposes it with CDD, and intervenes in scale space so model responses can be tested against physically coherent changes.

03 · 3D density reconstruction

Volume Density Mapper

Li & Zhao introduced the Volume Density Mapper to reconstruct 3D density fields from 2D column-density maps through constrained-diffusion multiscale components. The closely related MDR work by Zhao et al. provides plane-of-sky volume-density and structural-width maps with the same transparent scale-density logic.

column density→CDD / MDR→volume density + width→3D reconstruction

04 · Magnetic regulation

A unified MHD view: gradual B–ρ transition

This program asks how magnetic importance changes continuously across the multiphase ISM. The earlier B–ρ slope work connects magnetic-field scaling to Alfvénic state; the unified 2026 work tests that continuous picture from diffuse pulsar measurements to dense Zeeman regimes.

05 · Density & gravity

Multi-fractal density structure

Applying MDR locally turns density structure into a spatially resolved density–scale relation. In Orion A, the connected Integral Shaped Filament does not follow one universal local density exponent; progressively steeper scaling toward active regions supports a gravity-driven differential-evolution picture.

06 · Multiscale morphology

Scale-Vector Alignment

SVA compares the relative scale composition of two fields locally and their corresponding component maps scale by scale. It answers two complementary questions: where do two tracers share structure, and at what physical scales does that correspondence occur?
